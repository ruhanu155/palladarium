| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| First season | Days 1–7 | Small ponds, reeds, nest boxes, junk pile | Tadpoles; laser level and waders; first crafted items |

**The finale goal: Reference site.**

- **Earned when:** for one full season, every native species on the cove list is rated within or above its Park Service allotment in the season report, using survey estimates no older than 14 days, with invasive cover under 5% and a stream health score of 7 or more on every reach. - **Reward:** the special year card with the dawn flyover, +200 reputation, and a gold version of the cove's badge.
- The game continues afterward.

### The cove paper

**Version:** v0.5.

**How it plays.** Years of field data can become a paper. At the RV computer the player opens the Paper app, picks a topic with at least 2 years of data, picks the 3 charts that tell the story and the claim they support, and submits. A journal answers within 7 days. More years and cleaner data reach a better journal, and a published paper keeps being cited.

**Topics.** One paper per topic per game year.

| Topic | Data it uses |
| --- | --- |
| Nature calendar | The journal's first-of-year dates: first frog call, first leaf-out, first firefly, first frost |
| Stream health | Stream health scores per reach from kick-net samples |
| Population trends | Survey and mark-recapture estimates with their ranges |
| BioBlitz | Yearly species totals and station counts |
| Returns | Tag reports and banded birds that came back |
| Night sky | Dark-sky scores, moth diversity scores and meteor counts |

**Hands-on.** The app draws 6 charts from the player's own records and offers 3 claims: one the data supports, one too strong, one it doesn't support. The player picks 3 charts and 1 claim. Writing takes 2 game hours at the computer and runs as a time-lapse.

**Score.** `data_score`, 0–100, is the sum of three parts. Years: 12 per full year of data on that topic, capped at 60. Coverage: 30 × the share of seasons in the span with fresh records for that topic. Clean: 10, minus 2 for each stale, faked or incomplete record in the span, never below 0. A paper needs at least 2 years (8 seasons) of data with 4 or more fresh seasons. A claim the data doesn't support, or charts that don't show it, sends the paper back whatever the score.

| Score | Result | Reward |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Under 40, or a wrong claim | Revise and resubmit | None; resubmit after 7 days |
| 40–59 | Regional journal | $150 research grant, +10 reputation, 5 SC |
| 60–79 | National journal | $300, +20 reputation, 8 SC |
| 80–100 | Top journal | $600, +40 reputation, 10 SC |

With 2 years of data the best possible score is 64, so a top journal needs about 4 years.

**Citations.** Each published paper is cited every season: regional 1, national 2, top 4 times. Each citation is +1 reputation at the season report, capped at +8 a season across all papers. The year card's Cited line counts them, and the Chronicle records each paper ("Year 4, fall: the trout paper is published in a national journal").

**Mistakes.** A returned paper costs the 2 game hours and a 7-day wait. Stale or faked records lower the clean score, so the paper lands in a lower journal. Nothing else is lost.

**Items.** Paper reprint (`fieldstation:paper_reprint`): earned with each published paper; a framed first page that hangs in the RV.

**Hooks.** The journal's first-date records; the stream health score; survey estimates and mark and recapture; the BioBlitz; Tags that travel; Dark sky; the stale-data rule (Reputation); the Chronicle and the year card; science credits.

**GameTests.**

- `fieldstation:progression/paper_score_tiers`: a Stream health paper with 3 full years, full coverage and 1 incomplete record scores 36 + 30 + 8 = 74, is published in a national journal, and pays $300, +20 reputation and 8 SC.
- `fieldstation:progression/unsupported_claim_returns_paper`: the same data with the unsupported claim is returned with no reward, and resubmission opens 7 days later.

## Failure, recovery and difficulty

**No game over.** The worst that happens is a setback the land can recover from:

| Setback | How the player recovers |
| --- | --- |
| Out of money | Species grants keep paying each morning; the land trust offers a $200 emergency grant once a season |
| A pond fails | Ponds can be rebuilt; species recolonize from other habitats |
| A species dies out on the plot | It can arrive again by dispersal or through a lab release |
| Reputation drops | Tiers never drop; good work earns it back |

**Difficulty presets.**

| Preset | Changes |
| --- | --- |
| Relaxed | Erosion ×0.5, dams never breach, grants ×2, no disease |
| Standard | The numbers in this blueprint |
| Realist | Erosion ×1.5, stricter permits, disease and invasives at full strength, no emergency grant |
| Sandbox | Unlimited money, everything unlocked; for building and experimenting |

Each preset can be fine-tuned with sliders for erosion, rain, grant size and invasive pressure.

**Presets and the storyteller.** Presets also set how much trouble the storyteller can send:

| Preset | Budget refill per day | Large events warned |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Relaxed | 5 | 3 days ahead |
| Standard | 8 | 2 days ahead |
| Realist | 12 | 1 day ahead |
| Sandbox | Off unless turned on | — |
