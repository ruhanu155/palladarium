# Field Station: one-page canon

The north star. When any tab disagrees with this page, this page wins until Ruhan changes it.

**Logline.** You park a field station RV in a damaged Appalachian cove and bring it back to life with your own hands: one pond, one creek, one species at a time.

**Player fantasy.** Part restoration ecologist, part field biologist, part homesteader. You read the land, reshape it, and watch life answer.

**The place.** A 200 × 200 block Blue Ridge cove: Laurel Spring, the trout creek Hemlock Run, a silted farm pond, an eroding logging road, an old pasture, dying hemlocks, an old homeplace. Logged and grazed a lifetime ago, half grown back.

**What you do.**

- Dig, haul, pack and plant.
- Survey, catch and release.
- Test water.
- Care for animals in the RV lab.
- Craft habitat.
- Answer storms.

**Pillars**, ranked:

1. The land answers back.
2. Respect the player's time.
3. Play, don't follow.
4. Hands-on, never a menu press.
5. Everything readable.
6. Real detail.
7. Calm but not static.
8. Grow without an end.

**The loop.** Wake to a briefing. Work the land. Meet the day's event. Survey at dusk. Sleep. Each season ends with the Park Service season report, and each year with a year card.

**What it isn't.**

- No combat, no hostile mobs.
- No vanilla hunger. Food and sleep needs are a player option: off, comfort or real.
- No villages.
- No forced story.
- Solo for now.
- Animals are never killed by the player, and wild animals are never sold.

**Tone and look.** Quiet, honest, and plain-spoken. Clean, flush blocks in soft natural colors. Real calls and running water instead of music. Small wins feel big.

**Finished means.** v1.0, The Cove: the cove restored from damaged to healthy in about 6–8 game years (30–40 hours), ending in the reference-site finale. Play continues after the finale.

**Tech.** A NeoForge mod (mod id fieldstation) for Minecraft Java 26.2, built on Ruhan's Mac, with the code in git on GitHub. The simulation runs in its own data on the server; the world is its view.
