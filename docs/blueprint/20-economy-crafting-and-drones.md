# Economy, crafting and drones

&#91;embedded content: economy loop · land, habitat, science, gear\]

The economy runs in one loop. Work on the land grows habitat, habitat brings species, species and good data earn money and standing, and those buy or unlock the gear for the next job.

**Three ways to get things.**

| Kind of item | How you get it | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Habitat structures (nest boxes, brush and log piles, wattle, check dams, live stakes) | Craft, sometimes with a small bought part | Built from your own land's materials |
| Water-control hardware (pond leveler, culvert, gabions, fish ladder) | Craft from bought parts plus gathered stone and logs | Real restoration is part store-bought, part local |
| Hand tools (shovel, tamper, trowel in the starting kit; rake, pick, scythe, saw bought) | Buy | Made of steel; not craftable |
| Instruments and electronics (laser level, camera traps, loggers, GPS, PCR kit) | Buy, or earn | Manufactured precision gear |
| Lab stations | Buy. The vivarium glass is bought, then its layers are built by hand, as in Mistwall | Equipment |
| Consumables (reagents, bottles, hardware kits, wire, pipe, fuel) | Buy | Used up |
| Seed of species on your land | Gather with the seed collector | Free, from the land |
| Seed of species you don't have yet | Buy | Brings in new natives |
| Rentals (mini excavator, plate compactor) | Rent for one day; delivered by truck, and the rental key summons the machine at the drone pad | Too big for the drone |
| Prototype and agency gear (survey drone, thermal camera, hydrophone, weather station) | Earn only | Rewards for real achievement |
| Animals | Almost never bought; they arrive on their own | Conservation rules |

## Currencies

| Currency | Earned from | Spent on | Rules |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cash ($) | Species grants, goals, contracts, material orders, reports, plug and seed sales | Shop, rentals, fuel, permits, fines, neighbor agreements, the cove easement; travel and land after v1.0 | Can go to zero, never below |
| Reputation | Good work (see the Progression tab) | Nothing; it unlocks tiers | Tiers never drop |
| Science credits (SC) | High-quality data | The Research exchange (earned-only gear) | Can't be bought, sold or traded |

**How science credits are earned.**

| Source | Credits |
| --- | --- |

| First record of a species | 2 SC |
| A contract finished with top data quality | 5–15 SC |
| A multi-week study chain (several linked contracts) | 30 SC |
| The Appalachian badge (every cove species logged) | 50 SC |
| A placement with a complete studbook record | 3 SC |
| Solve a species mystery record | 5 SC |
| Solve a reach mystery | 3 SC |
| A tagged monarch is recovered | 3 SC |
| A banded bird returns to its reach | 1 SC |
| A good storm spotter report of quarter-size hail or a flash rise of 4+ levels | 3 SC |
| A record BioBlitz total | 5 SC |

Cash rewards effort. Science credits reward doing science well, so careful players get the best gear, not just rich ones.

## Gathering materials

| Material | Where it comes from | Tool | When | Used for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Branches | Brush clearing; limbs down after storms | Brush cutter or by hand | Any time | Brush piles, wattle |
| Logs | Felled trees, fallen snags | Saw | Any time | Log piles, log check dams, fish ladders, planks |
| Willow whips | Dormant willow and dogwood stems | Hatchet | Winter only | Live stakes, beaver dam analogs |
| Reed stems | Dead cattail and rush stems | Scythe | Fall and winter only | Bee hotels |
| Plant fiber | Mowing | Scythe | After midsummer | Mulch, coir logs, compost |
| Leaf litter | Raking under trees | Rake | Fall | Compost, mulch |
| Cobble | Rock outcrops; broken stone with the pick | Pick, rock bar | Any time | Riprap, check dams, gabions, fish ladders |
| Clay, sand, gravel, topsoil | Digging | Shovel | Any time | Building and lining |
| Silt | Dredging | Shovel | Any time | Fertile topsoil |
| Scrap metal and wire | Junk pile and old fences on the Appalachian plot | By hand | Until it runs out | Recycled into hardware kits at the workbench (4 scrap → 1 kit) |
| Seed | Mature native plants | Seed collector | Each species' seed season | Planting, selling |

**Gathering has a habitat cost.**

- Cutting reeds removes cover until they regrow. Cutting them in spring or summer disturbs nests and costs reputation, which is why reed cutting is a fall and winter job.
- Felling a tree opens the canopy.
- Hauling away deadwood removes log habitat.

The journal tracks what was taken, and the ecology responds. Taking only what you need is part of the game.

**Vanilla recipes.**

| Kept | Removed |
| --- | --- |
| Planks, sticks, slabs, stairs, fences, gates, doors, ladders, chests, barrels, signs, lanterns | All tools, weapons and armor; TNT; beds (the RV bed is the only bed) |

**Unlocking recipes.** A recipe appears in the recipe book when it is unlocked in one of these ways:

- by reaching a reputation tier
- by finishing a goal
- through a how-to sheet sent with a contract reward
- by examining a built example (for instance, a neighbor's check dam) with the field journal open

**The delivery drone.**

- **Arrival.** The drone lands within 10 seconds of the order and lowers the crate.
- **Where.** The RV drone pad is free. A field beacon ($30, placed anywhere on the plot) costs $5 a delivery, so 16 blocks of gravel can land right at the dam site.
- **Payload.** One crate holds 27 item slots or 64 heavy blocks. Bigger orders take extra trips, 5 seconds each.
- **Weather.** The drone is grounded in thunderstorms and in wind above 40 km/h. Orders queue until it clears. This is the only time an order waits.
- **Noise.** Landing scares wildlife within 16 blocks for 1 game minute, so beacons belong away from nests and ponds.
- **Returns.** Unused items go back within 7 days for 50% of the price.

**Prices.** The base price list stays fixed. Each week, one category is 20% off. Seed is 20% cheaper in fall, and plugs are 20% dearer in spring.

**Crews.** Crews keep long simple chores under the 5-minute rule.

| Crew | Cost | When | Jobs |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Volunteer crew | Free | Once a game week, posted on the Orders tab | 2–4 people for one day |
| Hired crew | $60 a day per person | Any day | The same jobs |

Crews only do simple jobs from a plan's job list: hauling, planting, pulling weeds. They never do skilled work: stream work, the lab, surveys or machines.

## Selling and orders

Plant plugs and seed sell on the open market. Everything else sells only by filling an order. That keeps selling tied to real demand and stops endless farming of one item.

| Item | Price | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Native plant plugs | $3 each | 20 per buyer per week |
| Seed packets | $2–$8 by species | 10 per species per week |
| Bluebird or wood duck box | $25 | Orders only |
| Bat box | $35 | Orders only |
| Bee hotel | $15 | Orders only |
| Compost topsoil | $4 a block | Orders only |
| Dredged silt | $2 a block | Orders only |
| Captive-bred animals | Not sold in v1.0 | Placements only (phase 1) |
| Honey | $8 a jar | Orders only |
| Heirloom apples | $2 each | Orders only |
| Heirloom scion wood | $8 each | Orders only, in winter |

**Orders.** Orders live in the Orders tab of the Contracts app (its other tab is Contracts). Schools, the land trust and local gardeners post requests, for example "12 bluebird boxes for the elementary school", "20 heirloom apples for the farmers' market" or "6 Magnum Bonum scions for a heritage orchard". Orders pay 20% over the listed price and give reputation.

**What buyers want by season.**

| Season | Most-wanted items |
| --- | --- |
| Winter | Nest boxes, bat boxes |
| Spring | Plant plugs, compost |
| Summer | Bee hotels, bat boxes |
| Fall | Seed packets |

Orders come in slowly, so crafting for sale is a side income, not a factory.

**Regional seed bank.** From v0.4, seed from the seed vault can be shipped to the regional seed bank (Additions → Seed vault). A shipment is 8 packets of one species and earns 3 SC, or 6 SC for a species on the bank's posted wanted list. Up to 4 shipments a week. Shipments pay no cash.

## Faucets, sinks and fair play

| Money in | Money out |
| --- | --- |
| Species grants (the steady base) | Shop purchases |
| Goal rewards | Rentals |
| Contracts | Fuel and travel |
| Orders and market sales | Permits |
| Reports | Upkeep: reagents, feed, generator fuel |
| Stormwater credit | Fines |
| Emergency grant (once a season) | Upgrades (the Upgrades tab: tools, machines, buildings, drones, vehicles, comfort; plus the larger RV); neighbor agreements; the cove easement ($6,000, tuning value; Station director: locks the cove and pays +150 reputation) |

Income targets per stage are in the Progression and economy tab.

**Rules that keep it fair.**

- A species earns its grant only while it has a viable population on the plot: a breeding pair or 2+ individuals. Visitors such as the heron count if seen within the last 7 days.
- Animals held in the lab don't earn grants.
- Stocked animals (fish bought with a stocking permit) earn no grant for their first season, so stocking can't be farmed.
- Contract data is checked against the simulation. Inflated counts are caught and cost reputation.
- Market sales have weekly limits. Everything else needs an order.
- Gathering has a habitat cost, so materials can't be farmed for free.
- Reward crates are fixed, and science credits can't be traded.
